About & Methodology

Data sources, collection methods and definitions

About this portal

The Manufacturing Industry Portal is the public-facing information service of the Manufacturing Business Information System, operated by the Ethiopian Ministry of Industry. It publishes aggregated, non-confidential statistics collected from registered manufacturing enterprises nationwide.

Data collection methodology

Data is collected through periodic surveys administered by woreda, zonal, and regional officers using the Enterprise & Data Collection mobile and web applications, then verified through a multi-level review and approval workflow before publication.

Update frequency

Core indicators are refreshed quarterly. The enterprise registry and GIS map are updated continuously as new registrations and verifications are approved.

Contact & data requests

Researchers and institutions requiring micro-data access may submit a formal data request to the Ministry of Industry Statistics Directorate.
